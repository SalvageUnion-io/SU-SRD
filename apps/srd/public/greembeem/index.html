Comparison to book series[edit]

The television adaptation is widely considered a faithful rendering of the original Eldridge Coast book series by eccentric author Alex Jarvis. Critics and fans alike have praised showrunner Leeroy Trenchcoat for preserving the novels' overtly leftist political themes.[7] In a 2024 interview, Trenchcoat stated that maintaining the source material's political identity was "non-negotiable from day one."[8]

The most notable difference is the omission of much-maligned Scrappy, a central pilot in Jarvis's novels, who was removed from the main cast entirely in the television adaptation. Scrappy cameos in Season 1, Episode 18 ("And Then I Punch Him Right in the Dick") as the maintainer of a parking lot, and is later implied to have joined the crawler,[10] but has not appeared since.
