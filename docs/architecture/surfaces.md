# Surfaces and enforcement

Where a rule is enforced, and what the app may do on its own ([surfaces](../ARCHITECTURE.md#surfaces): ADR-007, ADR-009, ADR-021). A rule's enforcement is a function of the surface's mode, not of the rule alone. Classify both axes (mode and rule class) before building any rules feature.

1. **§1 Modes** — The border between modes is the lifecycle transaction: a state change the rules gate behind a cost or procedure.
    - Guided Creation, the Wizard: enforces creation rules, a how-to guide for making a legal entity.
    - Free Edit, the Live Sheet: no lifecycle enforcement; edit the end state directly, override caps. It is deliberately the least-enforced surface: a hard block there beyond structural coherence is a change to this spec, not a bug fix.
    - Guided Play, Play's player screen (`/play/pilots/$pilotId`, Pilot + Mech + Crawler live): enforces play through interactive rules layers that teach as they enforce.
    - Adjudicate, Play's Mediator screen (`/play/games/$gameId/mediate`): surfaces rules, enforces nothing on player sheets.
    - Frozen, the public sheet (`/p/`) and a crewmate's sheet: read-only, live.
    - A Game's page (`/play/games/$gameId`) manages the table and is no mode: it writes no sheet. Play's screens: [play.md](play.md).
2. **§2 Rule placement** — Lifecycle transactions (use a system for EP, Heat and uses, Push, Scrap and TP costs, Downtime, craft, salvage, repair, upgrade, advancement) are enforced and interactive in the guided modes, bypassed in Free Edit; using a system is a Play act, though the Live Sheet may hand-edit a uses count. Structural coherence (refs resolve, type and containment) is hard in every mode; house-ruled arrangements of existing content are allowed, and new homebrew authoring surfaces are out of scope (showing what players already make is in, ADR-061). Quantitative caps (slot counts, derived maxima) are derived in guided modes, overridable in Free Edit with a callout and a retained baseline. Free state (HP, SP, Heat, EP, AP, Scrap, conditions, uses) is freely editable in Free Edit and moved only by enforced actions in Guided Play (Mediator-declared damage's SP-to-HP overflow is a Play transaction). Procedural adjudication is surfaced, never enforced.
3. **§3 The automation boundary** — In Guided Play the app automates bookkeeping and enforces the rules, but never changes an item's condition without the player. Auto-applied, because it is recoverable: Heat an action generates, AP and EP spent, uses decremented (`activationPatch` in `apps/web/src/lib/rules/actionPatches.ts`, one write-through patch); Heat clamped to the cap, with a Heat Check prompt when Heat is gained; SP damage from a Reactor Overload and other bookkeeping from the rules functions (`packages/salvageunion-reference/lib/rules/heatCheck.ts`); blocking an action whose resources are short or whose Heat would pass the cap. The player's act, because it is destructive: any condition change, destroying a Module or System from any source, a catastrophic meltdown. A roll that implies destruction surfaces the choice and never silently changes the sheet, which is why rules functions return results rather than apply them. Classify every new combat mechanic against this boundary before building it. In Free Edit the player edits conditions by hand.
4. **§4 Item condition** — Intact, then damaged, then destroyed, cycled only by the player through the card's `StatusBadge` (`cycleCondition` in `apps/web/src/lib/rules/mechItems.ts`); read-only sheets render a plain badge.
