# Contributions and provenance

How a stat's value is built and shown ([contributions](../ARCHITECTURE.md#contributions): ADR-029). The model is `packages/salvageunion-reference/lib/rules/contributions.ts` and `lib/schemas/objects/contributions.ts`; where a rule is enforced is the [surfaces](../ARCHITECTURE.md#surfaces) matrix.

1. **§1 One model** — A contribution is a source, an operation and a target, whether it raises Max SP, adds Burn 1 or bumps damage: `op` (the four cap keys, plus `addTrait`, `removeTrait`, `setRange`, `addDamage`), `target` (`self`, `pilot`, `pilotedMech`, `crawler` or a named item), `amount` (an integer, or a small expression over tech level), `stacks` (per installed copy), `voidWhen` (`damaged` or `destroyed`, with the current-value clamp) and `duration` (permanent by default, or `activated`, §4). Any content type may declare contributions: abilities, systems, modules, bays, crawler types, equipment. Do not fork the resolver: `resolveChoiceView.ts` applies trait, damage and range effects for every surface. Never infer a number from prose: author it where the text states one flatly, or record an exemption (§5).
2. **§2 Derivations return parts** — `StatBreakdown { contributions, derived, total, overridden }`; each contribution carries a label, source kind, signed amount and, where one exists, a ref to its source. Scalar functions (`mechMaxSP`, `pilotMaxHP`, …) are thin `.total` wrappers.
3. **§3 Provenance belongs to the value** — Any surface rendering a derived value can render its breakdown, opened on hover, focus and tap. Frozen sheets show it without the revert. Play leaves it to the sheet: Play's vitals (`PlayVital`) are numbers and − / +, and the sheet in Build is where a Play unit's provenance and overrides live, so `VitalGauge`'s compact rung draws neither. An override appends; it never replaces.
4. **§4 Duration effects are Play's** — `duration: activated` contributions (Squeeze It In, Hull Magnetiser) are switched on only by Play (a lifecycle transaction) and resolve against the pilot's seat on the Game (ADR-038 §2), never the persisted entity. Reference data says that an effect is activated and for how long; play state records when. The panel shows their remaining life.
5. **§5 The parity audit** — A CI check scans every record whose text states a mechanical change (cap, trait, damage, range) and requires either a structured contribution or a reasoned exemption. The exempt classes: chassis-integrated ("Integrated Cargo Bay: +10, to 16" is already the chassis stat), current-pool effects (Parasitic Membrane, Bite, Transfer move a pool without changing a cap), already-modelled mechanics (Rad Wave Generator through Major Injuries), effects of an effect (Mender's "+4 SP each time you heal") and grants at a moment (Pilot Bay's one-off +2 HP / +1 AP, a transaction).
6. **§6 Rules-bearing prose is marked** — In the app, which applies the rules, a rules-bearing paragraph leads with a mini ink AUTO stamp whose title (and screen-reader text) says the app applies it; the SRD prints plain prose. The app opts in through the entity surface's `appliesRules`. Unmarked prose that states a number is a visible coverage gap.
