# Play

The Play mode (ADR-051), **The Cantina** (`PRODUCT.play`). One screen to run a pilot, solo or in a Game, and the Mediator's version of it; the code is `apps/web/src/play/`. Play also owns the hub, each Game's page and invites (ADR-052). Play only plays: every unit opens its sheet in Build.

1. **§1 Routes** — `/play/` (`PlayLanding`: Your Games, each a Compact with chips, crew Minis (`gameCrew`) and doors (`gameDoors`); then Play solo), `/play/games/$gameId` (`GamePage`), `/play/invite/$token` (`InviteScreen`), `/play/pilots/$pilotId` (`PlayGate` → `PlayCockpit`), `/play/games/$gameId/mediate` (`PlayMediatorGate` → `PlayMediatorCockpit`), outside `/build` (ADR-062). Every retired address 301s there (`retiredTarget`); a gate that cannot open answers with `PlayRefusal`; a Game's Play (`LaunchPlay`) and Mediate (`GameBand`) lead there.
2. **§2 Frame** — `PlayFrame`, one layout, phone first (ADR-051 §2), filling the window below its top, never scrolling as a page; texture set once (bands speckled, ground flat; R10). Container queries on `.su-play-root` (`play/frame.css`): from `lg` `PlayBar`, three columns, `PlayFoot` (the connection line); below it tabs (§11). Panes (`playPanes.model.ts`) are listed in reading order; `usePlayWide`, read once, decides only what a reader is told and where the Situation switch sits, so crossing `lg` remounts no pane. `PlayCockpit` reads `usePlaySituation(target)` and `usePlaySide` once for every pane.
3. **§3 Situation** — `PlaySituationSwitch`: On foot and Boarded are the seat's `mount` (`useSeatAt`, solo seat included); Downtime is the Game's `downtime` row, locked for players, or the solo seat's own (`usePlayDowntime`, one `downtime` API keyed on `gameId` or null). Downtime wins over the mount (`situationOf`).
4. **§4 Units** — `PlayUnitsColumn` (`PlayPilotUnit`, `PlayMechUnit`, `PlayCrawlerUnit`, `PlayHangarMech`; `unitLiveness`). Live units get − / + (`PlayVital`, `VitalGauge`'s steppers; numbers only: provenance and overrides are the sheet's, ADR-029 §3); reference units are dashed and read-only, Board their one control. Pilot always live, mech while Boarded, crawler in solo Downtime or for the Mediator. Boarded, Effects use `seats.toggleEffect`. Sheet opens the unit's sheet in Build (`useOpenSheet`).
5. **§5 Actions** — `PlayActionsColumn` / `usePlayActions`: the Scene (`PlayScene`), then one list (`buildActionList`) from `useActionsDeck`, sorted Turn, Short, Long, Reaction, Free, Downtime, with a type filter and Find; On hand, then Not on hand and why (`PlayActionsList`): contentful Minis, a `MiniGrid` (`PlayActionRow`; others `playLine.model.ts`). Use resolves inline as its body (`PlayResolveSteps`) on the seat's `resolving`; a roll there is its table's `PlayRollInstrument` (R8). Area Salvage spends 1 Supply (`salvageAreas.spend`) and loads what fits (`areaSalvageToMech`, `…ToPilot`).
6. **§6 Downtime** — `PlayDowntimeColumn`: the ten guide steps as a track, the current step (`PlayDowntimeStep`) listing the patches from `game-domain/rules/downtime`, each skippable (ADR-007), then the Downtime actions (`PlayDowntimeActionRow`). A step is one op on the pilot with a stable id (`downtimeStepOpId`, ADR-063 §6), so Undo, the Mediator's Back or a reload never apply it twice.
7. **§7 Crew** — `PlayCrew` (`crewSeatLines`): other seats as Minis, each pair through `crewPair` (aboard, the mech's; on foot, a MECH chip), chips for the mount (R5), presence (a pilot is here while its owner's socket is open) and the vital that needs looking at; tap: `PlayCrewSheet`. The bar and each landing card count who is here (`hereCount`). Presence is the Game room's (`useGamePresence`, ADR-064): an open Play screen holds a socket to it, seat sockets count, watch sockets only listen; offline every column withdraws its controls and the bar says why (`playReadOnly`).
8. **§8 Tables and Feed** — `PlaySideColumn` holds the end column's panes: Crew (`PlayCrewSection`), then Tables and Feed (`PlayerTablesAndFeed`). `PlayTables` lists the Situation's tables and Find's as toggle `RollMini`s, the pressed one open (`PlayRollTable`); `PlayFeed` (`playFeedEntries`) merges `useGameFeed` with this screen's rolls, proposals accepted inline. Every roll in Play goes through `playRolls.model.ts` (`recordPlayRoll`, `reportingRoll`; an applied change `recordPlayChange`).
9. **§9 Discord** — `usePlayChannel.ts`. `/su bind` stores a `channelBindings` row (Game or solo) with the channel's name, which the channel button, the Post toggle and the landing show. `usePlayRollDispatch` writes each roll to the Game's log (never the Mediator's) and, with the screen's one Post toggle on (`postDefault`: the Mediator private) or the per-roll Post, `channelBindings.postRoll`, its result on its Feed line. `PlayChannelButton` in the bar; a chip on the landing's Game card.
10. **§10 Mediator** — `PlayMediatorCockpit` (`usePlayMediator` read once). The group session (`useGameSession`; glossary 10) starts on the hub, the band or ⋯. Bar: MEDIATOR, ONLINE "3 of 4 here" (`hereOf`), Scene / Downtime (`PlayGameStateSwitch`), Stop, the channel button, Tell the table (`TellTheTable`; phone: in ⋯, no MEDIATOR) and ⋯ (Game page, Reference in `SrdExplorer`; `PlayMediatorBarActions`). Left: "The table" (`PlayMediatorTable`), a seat per player (`PlayMediatorSeat`, `mediatorSeats`: a toggle Mini, presence, mech and ▲ vital as chips, every vital and what they resolve its body, ringed when `crewLines()` flags them; pressed aims Propose), then the crawler (`PlayCrawlerUnit`), live. Centre: the Scene (`PlayMediatorScene`, `sceneHour.model.ts`), the Opposition (`PlayMediatorOpposition`, `PlayNpcBox`: HP or SP through `mediator.updateNpc`, Morale, Mark important, Rename / Remove, + Add (`OppositionPicker`), Run an encounter (ADR-059: groups on `PlayNpcGroup`, Morale due, Bring in), Area (`salvageAreas.set`), a wreck's salvage (`PlayWreckSalvage`)) and the chosen NPC's actions (`PlayNpcActions`, `npcActions`: Use opens the NPC Action table); in Downtime `PlayMediatorDowntime` (`PlayDowntimeTrack`, who is done, Back through `downtime.setStep`, Advance, End Downtime, Upkeep through `payDowntimeUpkeep`, `playUpkeep.model.ts`, Deal income, Cantina Rumours). Right: `PlayMediatorSide`'s panes (`RunningBriefing`, Notes, Tables, private by default, and the Feed, `MediatorTablesAndFeed`; then `ProposeDock` and All proposals, `ProposalsList`, docked at the column's foot). The Mediator's rolls stay off the Game's log (`usePlayRollDispatch({ log: false })`) till posted.
11. **§11 Phone** — Below `lg`: the bar, the pinned row, the open pane (the only scroller; closed ones mounted, `hidden`), the tabs along the foot. The player's (`PLAYER_TABS`): Act (resolving inline; in Downtime the track and step box), Units, Tables (and Feed), Crew (a Game's), under the live vitals with − / + (`pinnedUnits`, `PlayPinnedVitals`: on foot HP and AP, Boarded the mech's SP, EP and Heat too, Downtime nothing) and the Situation switch (`fill`). The Mediator's: Table, Opposition (Downtime while it runs), Tables, Propose under the Scene / Downtime switch; a seat tapped opens Propose, focus in To. TABLE: `PlayTableMode`. Phone fits: `play/touch.css`.
12. **§12 Nesting** — The nesting rules N1–N8 (EDL E6; ADR-051 §11). Level 0 is the Play frame, a dialog or an overlay; level 1 a Compact or contentful card, a unit `TitledBox` or a Play list's Mini (`data-play-list`) in a pane, under a plain title or `Slab`; level 2 a Mini (N4) or plain content (resolve steps).
13. **§13 Tests** — The e2e and layout gates (#1632), helpers in `apps/web/e2e/_play.ts`. The signed-in `play-*.e2e.ts` run on every PR (`ci.yml` `build-web`, with the test sign-in seam) and nightly (`e2e-nightly.yml`); `a11y-signed-in.e2e.ts` nightly only. A roll reaching a bound channel is `src/worker/shared/__tests__/gameCalls.test.ts` and the bot's `relay.test.ts`.
14. **§14 State and rules** — Over the live sheet's entities, store and rules; never a second editor, never a fork (entities through the forms, tables `RollTableInstrument`). Vitals, uses and cargo persist on the records (`entityStore.update`); mount, range, `activeEffects`, resolving and ejected live on the seat (`src/rooms/seats.ts`); rolls are Game rows in the change log; maxima and crew status are derived, never stored. `lib/rules/actionPatches.ts` is a thin driver over `salvageunion-reference/lib/rules`, glue not math; a destructive result is the player's act (ADR-007).
    - **§14.1 Reactor** — Push, Heat Check, Vent (Heat 0, Vulnerable) and Shutdown are separate controls; a Meltdown waits for the player. Scene conditions scale its Heat (`game-domain/rules/sceneConditions`).
    - **§14.2 Action activation** — Activate → Roll → optional Push → Apply, the economy derived per action; `useActionsDeck` drives what `PlayResolveSteps` draws.
    - **§14.3 Damage → Critical** — Declared damage applies clamped; reaching 0 offers the Critical roll, whose SP/HP override alone auto-applies.
15. **§15 Proposals and Opposition** — The Propose dock: Target, Field (pilot HP/AP, mech SP/Heat), To, an optional reason (≤140 chars) and a preview; Setbacks (`SetbacksDialog`) drafts 2 SP/HP or +2 Heat; All proposals pages 20 at a time, each row's state in words, every Mediator's (`ops.sent`). The Opposition is hidden from players: each NPC its card with HP or SP and Morale, Morale stored on the NPC and never logged; at 0 it shows as down, never auto-removed, and Remove confirms. Important (p.255): EP, Heat, Heat Check for a mech; a Critical roll at 0. Tell the table is `changeLog.broadcast`, a feed row, not an op.
16. **§16 A Game's page** — `GamePage` (`play/games/`): everyone sees the Crew (`GameRoster`, one list; its head links out to Build; open seats are unassigned pilots; their seal assigns one to you, the table runner's menu any unit, ADR-030 §4) and Play for their pilot (a Mediator's too); the Mediator the crawler band, Mediate and Proposals sent; the Organiser the invite link (one per Game, Copy and Reset) and Hand over. Factions and Jobs (`GameLedger`): the Mediator's; Finish pays a job into the crawler. The Game names who mediates (Hand over, or Appoint when nobody does) and who organises, with Delete; Archive is the owner's.
17. **§17 Safety** — Safety (pp.12-13). `GameSafety`: the two boundary lists, unnamed. Stop (`StopSignButton`) pauses every open screen (`StopSignBanner`), naming nobody; its shower or the table runner lifts it. Opted in, it posts to Discord.
