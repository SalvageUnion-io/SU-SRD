# Play

The Play mode (ADR-051), named **The Cantina** (`PRODUCT.play`; pink header). One screen to run a pilot, solo or in a Game, and the Mediator's version of it; the code is `apps/web/src/play/`. Play also owns the hub, each Game's page and invites (ADR-052). Play only plays: every unit opens its sheet in Build.

1. **§1 Routes** — `/play/` (`PlayLanding`: Your Games, each a Compact with chips, crew Minis (`gameCrew`) and door; then Play solo), `/play/games/$gameId` (`GamePage`), `/invite/$token` (`InviteScreen`), `/play/$pilotId` (`PlayGate` → `PlayCockpit`), `/play/game/$gameId` (`PlayMediatorGate` → `PlayMediatorCockpit`). The retired `/games/$gameId`, `/dashboard/$pilotId` and `/mediator/$gameId` 301 there (`retiredTarget`); a gate that cannot open answers with `PlayRefusal`; a Game's Play (`LaunchPlay`) and Mediate (`GameBand`) lead there.
2. **§2 Frame** — `PlayFrame` on `PlayCanvas` (1280×800, scaled, no page scroll), its texture set once (bands speckled, ground flat; R10): `PlayBar`, then Units | Actions | Crew, Tables and Feed, then `PlayFoot` (`SavedIndicator`). One stylesheet bundle, `styles/play/play.css`, the canvas's rules first. `PlayCockpit` calls `usePlaySituation(target)` once and hands the same `Play` to the Situation switch and every column; `usePlayPresence` beats for the seat while it is open.
3. **§3 Situation** — `PlaySituationSwitch`: On foot and Boarded are the seat's `mount` (`useSeatAt`, solo seat included); Downtime is the Game's `downtime` row, locked for players, or the solo seat's own (`usePlayDowntime`, one `downtime` API keyed on `gameId` or null). Downtime wins over the mount (`situationOf`).
4. **§4 Units** — `PlayUnitsColumn` (`PlayPilotUnit`, `PlayMechUnit`, `PlayCrawlerUnit`, `PlayHangarMech`; `unitLiveness`). Live units get − / + (`PlayVital`, `VitalGauge`'s steppers; numbers only: provenance and overrides are the sheet's, ADR-029 §3); reference units are dashed and read-only, Board their one control. Pilot always live, mech while Boarded, crawler in solo Downtime or for the Mediator. Boarded, Effects use `seats.toggleEffect`. Sheet opens the unit's sheet in Build (`useOpenSheet`).
5. **§5 Actions** — `PlayActionsColumn` / `usePlayActions`: one list (`buildActionList`) from `useActionsDeck`, sorted Turn, Short, Long, Reaction, Free, Downtime, with a type filter and Find; On hand, then Not on hand and why (`PlayActionsList`): contentful Minis, a `MiniGrid` (`PlayActionRow`; others `playLine.model.ts`). Use resolves inline as its body (`PlayResolveSteps`) on the seat's `resolving`; a roll there is its table's `PlayRollInstrument` (R8).
6. **§6 Downtime** — `PlayDowntimeColumn`: the ten guide steps as a track, the current step (`PlayDowntimeStep`) listing the patches from `game-domain/rules/downtime`, each skippable (ADR-007), then the Downtime actions (`PlayDowntimeActionRow`). A step is claimed per pilot before it applies (`downtime.claimStep`: the Game's `claimed`, the solo seat's `doneSteps`), so Undo, the Mediator's Back or a reload never apply it twice.
7. **§7 Crew** — `PlayCrew` (`crewSeatLines`): other seats as Minis, each pair through `crewPair` (aboard, the mech's; on foot, a MECH chip), chips for the mount (R5), presence (`lastSeenAt`, `isPresent`) and the vital that needs looking at; a tap opens `PlayCrewSheet`. The bar counts who is here (`hereCount`), and the landing each Game's (`seats.presence`, the seats' last beats only). Presence is on the seat, not a table of its own: each beat re-runs the Game's seat queries (`seats.forGame`, `crew.vitals`), throttled to one write per 20 s per player; offline every column withdraws its controls and the bar says why (`playReadOnly`).
8. **§8 Tables and Feed** — `PlaySideColumn` stacks Crew, Tables and Feed. `PlayTables` lists the Situation's tables and Find's as toggle `RollMini`s, the pressed one open (`PlayRollTable`); `PlayFeed` (`playFeedEntries`) merges `useGameFeed` with this screen's rolls, proposals accepted inline. Every roll in Play goes through `playRolls.model.ts` (`recordPlayRoll`, the Actions column's `reportingRoll`; an applied change is `recordPlayChange`).
9. **§9 Discord** — `usePlayChannel.ts`. `/su bind` stores a `channelBindings` row (Game or solo) with the channel's name, which the channel button, the Post toggle and the landing show. `usePlayRollDispatch` writes each roll to the Game's log (never the Mediator's) and, with the screen's one Post toggle on (`postDefault`: the Mediator private) or the per-roll Post, `channelBindings.postRoll`, its result on its Feed line. `PlayChannelButton` in the bar; a chip on the landing's Game card.
10. **§10 Mediator** — `PlayMediatorCockpit` (`play/mediator/`, `usePlayMediator` read once). Bar: MEDIATOR, LIVE "3 of 4 here" (`hereOf`), Scene / Downtime (`PlayGameStateSwitch`), the channel button, Tell the table (`TellTheTable`) and ⋯ (Game page, Reference in `SrdExplorer`; `PlayMediatorBarActions`). Left: "The table" (`PlayMediatorTable`), a seat per player (`PlayMediatorSeat`, `mediatorSeats`: a toggle Mini, presence, mech and ▲ vital as chips, every vital and what they resolve its body, ringed when `crewLines()` flags them; pressed aims Propose), then the crawler as `PlayCrawlerUnit`, live. Centre: the Opposition (`PlayMediatorOpposition`, `PlayNpcBox`: HP or SP through `mediator.updateNpc`, Morale, Rename / Remove, + Add (`OppositionPicker`), Run an encounter (ADR-058)) and the chosen NPC's actions (`PlayNpcActions`, `npcActions`: Use opens the NPC Action table); in Downtime `PlayMediatorDowntime` (`PlayDowntimeTrack`, who is done, Back through `downtime.setStep`, Advance, End Downtime, Upkeep through `payDowntimeUpkeep`, `playUpkeep.model.ts`, Cantina Rumours). Right: `PlayMediatorSide` (Tables, private by default; the Feed; `ProposeDock` and All proposals, `ProposalsList`). The Mediator's rolls stay off the Game's log (`usePlayRollDispatch({ log: false })`) and reach the players only when posted.
11. **§11 Phone** — Under 1280×800 (`isPhoneForm`, ADR-045 §1) `PlayCanvas` draws the screen's `phone` form instead, which fits the screen: `PlayPhone`, the bar (`PlayBar phone`: on two lines, "‹" alone), the pinned row, then the open tab's panel, the only thing that scrolls, and the tabs along the foot. Closed panels stay mounted (`TabPanel keepMounted`). The player's (`PlayCockpitPhone`): Act (`PlayActionsColumn`, resolving inline; in Downtime the track and the step box), Units, Tables (Tables and Feed), Crew (a Game's only), under the live vitals for the Situation (`pinnedUnits`, `PlayPinnedVitals`, gauges: on foot HP and AP, Boarded the mech's SP, EP and Heat too, Downtime nothing) and the Situation switch (`fill`, Boarded without the mech's name). The Mediator's (`PlayMediatorPhone`): Table, Opposition (Downtime while it runs), Tables, Propose under the Scene / Downtime switch; a seat tapped opens Propose with focus in To. The side's shared state is read once per screen (`usePlaySide`, `usePlayMediatorSide`), so the roll dispatch runs whichever tab is open. Touch sizes: `.su-play-root--phone`.
12. **§12 Nesting** — The nesting rules N1–N8 (EDL E6; ADR-051 §11). Level 0 is the Play canvas, a dialog or an overlay; level 1 a Compact or contentful card, a unit `TitledBox` or a Play list's Mini (`data-play-list`) in a column, under a plain title or `Slab`; level 2 a Mini (N4) or plain content (resolve steps).
13. **§13 Tests** — The e2e and layout gates (#1632), helpers in `apps/web/e2e/_play.ts` (`openPlay`, `playDoor` off the landing, `board`, `expectNoPageScroll` at 1280×800, 1024×700 and 390×844, `axeViolations` on the live page). Signed in, on every PR (`ci.yml` `build-web`, with the test sign-in seam) and again nightly (`e2e-nightly.yml`): `play-join.e2e.ts` (a Game end to end), `play-turn.e2e.ts` (board → use → roll → apply, canvas and phone), `play-mediator.e2e.ts` (propose → accept; Begin Downtime → a player's step → Advance), `play-solo.e2e.ts` (each solo Situation), `sibling-heights.e2e.ts`'s Play test; `a11y-signed-in.e2e.ts` (landing, player, Mediator) runs nightly only. Signed out, on every PR (`ci.yml` `build-web`): the Play routes' bundle budgets, the landing and both sign-in refusals in `app-a11y.e2e.ts`, the landing in `sibling-heights.e2e.ts`. A roll reaching a bound channel is `test/convex/__tests__/playDiscordRelay.test.ts` (the bot's `/bot/bindChannel` door, a real local relay at `BOT_URL`).
14. **§14 State and rules** — Over the live sheet's entities, store and rules; never a second editor, never a fork (entities through the forms, tables `RollTableInstrument`). Vitals, uses and cargo persist on the records (`entityStore.update`); mount, range, `activeEffects`, resolving and ejected live on the seat (`convex/seats.ts`); rolls are Game rows in the change log; maxima and crew status are derived, never stored. `lib/rules/actionPatches.ts` is a thin driver over `salvageunion-reference/lib/rules`, glue not math; a destructive result is the player's act (ADR-007).
    - **§14.1 Reactor** — Push, Heat Check, Vent (Heat 0, Vulnerable) and Shutdown are separate controls; a Meltdown waits for the player.
    - **§14.2 Action activation** — Activate → Roll → optional Push → Apply, the economy derived per action; `useActionsDeck` drives what `PlayResolveSteps` draws.
    - **§14.3 Damage → Critical** — Declared damage applies clamped; reaching 0 offers the Critical roll, whose SP/HP override alone auto-applies.
15. **§15 Proposals and Opposition** — The Propose dock: Target, Field (pilot HP/AP, mech SP/Heat), To, an optional reason (≤140 chars, `changeLog.reason`) and a preview; All proposals pages 20 at a time, each row's state in words, every Mediator's (`proposals.sent`). The Opposition is hidden from players: each NPC its card with HP or SP and Morale, Morale stored on the NPC and never logged; at 0 it shows as down, never auto-removed, and Remove confirms. Tell the table is `proposals.broadcast`.
16. **§16 A Game's page** — `GamePage` (`play/games/`): everyone sees the Crew (`GameRoster`, one list; its head links out to Build; open seats are unassigned pilots; their seal assigns one to you, the table runner's row menu any pilot or mech, ADR-030 §4) and Play for their pilot; the Mediator the crawler band, Mediate and Proposals sent; the Organizer the invite link (one per Game, Copy and Reset) and Hand over. The Game names who mediates (Hand over, or Appoint when nobody does) and who organises, with Delete; Archive is the owner's.
