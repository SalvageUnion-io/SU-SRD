# Play

The Play mode (ADR-051), as built: one screen to run a pilot, solo or in a Game, and the Mediator's version of it. The `dashboard-cockpit` milestone (tracker #1633) built it and retired the Dashboard and the Mediator dashboard; the code is `apps/web/src/components/play/`. Wireframes: [`docs/wireframes/dashboard-cockpit/`](../wireframes/dashboard-cockpit/).

1. **§1 Routes** — `/play/` (`PlayLanding`: Your Games, Solo, Discord), `/play/$pilotId` (`PlayGate` → `PlayCockpit`), `/play/game/$gameId` (`PlayMediatorGate` → `PlayMediatorCockpit`). The retired `/dashboard/$pilotId` and `/mediator/$gameId` redirect to the last two (`retiredRoutes.test.ts`); a gate that cannot open answers with `PlayRefusal`; the Game hub's Play (`LaunchPlay`) and Mediate (`GameBand`) lead there too.
2. **§2 Frame** — `PlayFrame` on `PlayCanvas` (1280×800, scaled, letterboxed, no page scroll; `playCanvas.model.ts`): `PlayBar`, then Units | Actions | Crew, Tables and Feed, then `PlayFoot` (`SavedIndicator`). Two stylesheet bundles: the canvas's `.su-dash-*` (`styles/cantina/dashboard.css`), then `apps/web/src/styles/cantina/play.css`. `PlayCockpit` calls `usePlaySituation(target)` once and hands the same `Play` (`situation.model.ts`) to the Situation switch and every column; `usePlayPresence` beats for the seat while it is open.
3. **§3 Situation** — `PlaySituationSwitch`: On foot and Boarded are the seat's `mount` (`usePlaySeat` over `useSeatAt`, solo seat included); Downtime is the Game's `downtime` row (`useGameDowntime`), locked for players, or the solo seat's own (`usePlayDowntime` wraps both). Downtime wins over the mount (`situationOf`).
4. **§4 Units** — `PlayUnitsColumn` (`PlayPilotUnit`, `PlayMechUnit`, `PlayCrawlerUnit`, `PlayHangarMech`; `unitLiveness`). Live units get − / + (`PlayVital`) through `entityStore.update`; reference units are dashed and read-only, Board their one control. Pilot always live, mech while Boarded, crawler in solo Downtime or for the Mediator. Sheet ⤢ opens `PlaySheetOverlay` over Play.
5. **§5 Actions** — `PlayActionsColumn` / `usePlayActions`: one list (`buildActionList`) driven by `useActionsDeck`, sorted Turn, Short, Long, Reaction, Free, Downtime, with a type filter and Find; On hand, then Not on hand with the reason (`PlayActionsList`). Use resolves inline in the action's `PlayActionRow` (`PlayResolveSteps`) on the seat's `resolving`; `PlayReactorRow` and `PlayMountRow` are rows.
6. **§6 Downtime** — `PlayDowntimeColumn`: the ten guide steps as a track, the current step (`PlayDowntimeStep`) listing the patches from `cantina-domain/rules/downtime`, each skippable (ADR-007), then the Downtime actions (`PlayDowntimeActionRow`). A step is claimed per pilot before it applies (the solo seat's `doneSteps`, the Game's `downtime.claimStep`), so Undo, the Mediator's Back or a reload never apply it twice.
7. **§7 Crew** — `PlayCrew` (`crewSeatLines`): other seats with presence (the seat's `lastSeenAt`, `isPresent`), mount and the vital that needs looking at; a tap opens `PlayCrewSheet` read-only. The bar counts who is here (`hereCount`), and the landing each Game's (`seats.presence`, the seats' last beats only). Presence is on the seat, not a table of its own: each beat re-runs the Game's seat queries (`seats.forGame`, `crew.vitals`), throttled to one write per 20 s per player; offline every column withdraws its controls and the bar says why (`playReadOnly`).
8. **§8 Tables and Feed** — `PlaySideColumn` stacks Crew, Tables and Feed. `PlayTables` pins the Situation's tables (`PlayRollTable`), Find for the rest; `PlayFeed` (`playFeedEntries`) merges `useGameFeed` with this screen's rolls, proposals accepted inline. Every roll in Play goes through `playRolls.model.ts` (`recordPlayRoll`, the Actions column's `reportingRoll`; an applied change is `recordPlayChange`).
9. **§9 Discord** — `usePlayChannel.ts`. `/su bind` stores a `channelBindings` row (Game or solo) with the channel's name, which the channel button, the Post toggle and the landing show (`#salvage-run`; whose channel it is on a row that kept none). `usePlayRollDispatch` writes each roll to the Game's log (never the Mediator's) and, with the screen's one Post toggle on (`postDefault`: the Mediator private) or the per-roll Post, `channelBindings.postRoll`, its result on its Feed line. `PlayChannelButton` in the bar, `PlayDiscordSection` on the landing.
10. **§10 Mediator** — `PlayMediatorCockpit` (`components/play/mediator/`, `usePlayMediator` read once and handed to every column). Bar: MEDIATOR, LIVE "3 of 4 here" (`hereOf`), Scene / Downtime (`PlayGameStateSwitch`), the channel button, Tell the table (`TellTheTable`) and ⋯ (Game page, Reference in `SrdExplorer`; `PlayMediatorBarActions`). Left: "The table" (`PlayMediatorTable`), a seat per player (`PlayMediatorSeat`, `mediatorSeats`: presence, the mount as a Token, every live vital, what they are resolving, ▲ and the ring when `crewLines()` flags them; a tap aims Propose), then the crawler as `PlayCrawlerUnit`, live. Centre: the Opposition (`PlayMediatorOpposition`, `PlayNpcBox`: HP or SP through `mediator.updateNpc`, Morale, Rename / Remove, + Add with `OppositionPicker`) and the chosen NPC's actions (`PlayNpcActions`, `npcActions`, rolled on the NPC Action table); in Downtime `PlayMediatorDowntime` (`PlayDowntimeTrack`, who is done, Back through `downtime.setStep`, Advance, End Downtime, Upkeep through `payDowntimeUpkeep`, `playUpkeep.model.ts`, Cantina Rumours). Right: `PlayMediatorSide` (Tables, private by default; the Feed; `ProposeDock` and All proposals, `ProposalsList`). The Mediator's rolls stay off the Game's log (`usePlayRollDispatch({ log: false })`) and reach the players only when posted.
11. **§11 Phone** — Below the scale floor `PlayCanvas` draws the screen's `phone` form instead, which fits the screen: `PlayPhone`, the bar (`PlayBar phone`: on two lines, "‹" alone), the pinned row, then the open tab's panel, the only thing that scrolls, and the tabs along the foot. Every panel stays mounted, hidden, while closed (`TabPanel keepMounted`). The player's (`PlayCockpitPhone`): Act (`PlayActionsColumn`, resolving inline; in Downtime the track and the step box), Units, Tables (Tables and Feed), Crew (a Game's only), under the live vitals for the Situation (`pinnedUnits`, `PlayPinnedVitals`: on foot HP and AP, Boarded the mech's SP, EP and Heat too, Downtime nothing) and the Situation switch (`fill`, Boarded without the mech's name). The Mediator's (`PlayMediatorPhone`): Table, Opposition (Downtime while it runs), Tables, Propose under the Scene / Downtime switch; a seat tapped opens Propose with focus in To. The side's shared state is read once per screen (`usePlaySide`, `usePlayMediatorSide`), so the roll dispatch runs whichever tab is open. Touch sizes are `.su-dash-phone`'s; the layout's rules over the columns are `play/phone.css`.
12. **§12 Nesting** — The nesting rules N1–N7 (ruleset §5; ADR-051 §11). Level 0 is the Play canvas, a dialog or an overlay; level 1 a compact Row or Card or a unit `TitledBox` in a column, under a plain title or `Slab`; level 2 a Token (`.su-entity-token-list` for a list, N4) or plain content, such as the resolve steps in an action Row (Row's `leading` slot).
13. **§13 Tests** — The e2e and layout gates (#1632), helpers in `apps/web/e2e/_play.ts` (`openPlay`, `playDoor` off the landing, `board`, `expectNoPageScroll` at 1280×800, the scale floor 794×497 and 390×844, `axeViolations` on the live page). Signed in, on every PR (`ci.yml` `build-web`, whose build carries the test sign-in seam) and again nightly (`e2e-nightly.yml`): `play-turn.e2e.ts` (board → use → roll → apply, canvas and phone), `play-mediator.e2e.ts` (propose → accept; Begin Downtime → a player's step → Advance), `play-solo.e2e.ts` (each solo Situation), `sibling-heights.e2e.ts`'s Play test (the bar, unit boxes, action rows); `a11y-signed-in.e2e.ts` (landing, player, Mediator) runs nightly only. Signed out, on every PR (`ci.yml` `build-web`): the Play routes' bundle budgets, the landing and both sign-in refusals in `cantina-a11y.e2e.ts`, the landing in `sibling-heights.e2e.ts`. A roll reaching a bound channel is `test/convex/__tests__/playDiscordRelay.test.ts` (the bot's `/bot/bindChannel` door, a real local relay at `CANTINA_BOT_URL`): binding needs a Discord-linked account, which the test sign-in seam has none of.
