# Addresses

Where everything lives on `salvageunion.io` ([addresses](../ARCHITECTURE.md#addresses): ADR-048, ADR-062).

1. **§1 Three roots, one per mode** — Reference at `/`, the Training Bay at `/build/`, Play at `/play/`, all served by the `su-srd` Worker; the old app host 301s by zone rule. The reference owns the root, so no schema id may take a root a site page, a `public/` file, the app or a redirect already holds (`rootCollisions.test.ts`).
2. **§2 One app, two roots** — `apps/web` stays one client app. Its routes keep their in-app strings, and Play's are spelled as their site paths (`/play/…`), so one pair maps both ways: `appPath(route)` (a Play route as it is, anything else under `/build`) and `routeInApp(pathname)` (`game-domain/appAddress.ts`). The router's `rewrite`, the Worker, the service worker's navigation rule, e2e and the Discord bot all read that pair; none spells `/build` itself.
3. **§3 Play** — `/play/` (the hub), `/play/games/:id` (a Game's page), `/play/games/:id/mediate` (its Mediator's Cockpit), `/play/pilots/:id` (a pilot's Cockpit), `/play/invite/:token`. Every path names a collection, then an id, then an action.
4. **§4 Build** — A unit's sheet is the plural of its kind and its id, as the wizards (`/build/pilots/new`) and the reference (`/chassis/mule/`) are: `/build/pilots/:id`, `/build/mechs/:id`, `/build/crawlers/:id`, `/build/npcs/:id` (`sheetPath`). Saved patterns are `/build/patterns/`.
5. **§5 Nothing old breaks** — Every retired address 301s in one hop, query kept (`retiredTarget`, `lib/redirects.ts`): `/build/play/…`, `/build/play/game/:id`, `/build/play/:pilotId`, `/build/invite/:token`, `/build/sheet/:kind/:id`, `/build/mechs/patterns`, and the older `/build/games…`, `/build/join/:code`, `/build/dashboard/:id`, `/build/mediator/:id`; retirement is read only under `/build`. The bot's `/sheet` still reads the old `/sheet/<kind>/<id>` links already posted.
