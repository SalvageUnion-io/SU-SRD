# Addresses

Where everything lives on `salvageunion.io` ([addresses](../ARCHITECTURE.md#addresses): ADR-048, ADR-062, ADR-066).

1. **§1 Three roots, one per area** — Reference at `/`, the Training Bay at `/build/`, the Games at `/games/`, all served by the `su-srd` Worker; the old app host 301s by zone rule. No schema id may take a root a page, a `public/` file, the app or a redirect holds (`rootCollisions.test.ts`). `/play` stays Worker-first only to 301 its retired addresses.
2. **§2 One app, two roots** — `apps/web` stays one client app. Its routes keep their in-app strings, and the Games' are spelled as their site paths (`/games/…`), so one pair maps both ways: `appPath(route)` (a Games route as it is, anything else under `/build`) and `routeInApp(pathname)` (`game-domain/appAddress.ts`). The router, Worker, service worker, e2e and the bot all read that pair; none spells `/build` itself.
3. **§3 Games** — `/games/` (the hub), `/games/:id` (a Game's page), `/games/:id/mediate` (its Mediator's Cockpit), `/games/invite/:token`. Every path names a collection, then an id, then an action.
4. **§4 Training Bay** — A unit's page is the plural of its kind and its id, as the wizards (`/build/pilots/new`) and the reference (`/chassis/mule/`) are: `/build/pilots/:id`, `/build/mechs/:id`, `/build/crawlers/:id`, `/build/npcs/:id` (`sheetPath`). A pilot's Play mode is `/build/pilots/:id/play` (`cockpitPath`). Saved patterns are `/build/patterns/`.
5. **§5 Nothing old breaks** — Every retired address 301s in one hop, query kept (`retiredTarget`, `lib/redirects.ts`): `/play/…` (`/play/pilots/:id` to the pilot's Play, the rest to `/games/…`), `/build/play/…`, `/build/play/game/:id`, `/build/play/:pilotId`, `/build/invite/:token`, `/build/sheet/:kind/:id`, `/build/mechs/patterns`, and the older `/build/games…`, `/build/join/:code`, `/build/dashboard/:id`, `/build/mediator/:id`. The bot's `/sheet` still reads the old `/sheet/<kind>/<id>` links already posted.
