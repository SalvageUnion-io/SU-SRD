# The Hard Rules

Each law of [ruleset.md](ruleset.md), [EDL](entity-display-language.md), [chips-and-actions.md](chips-and-actions.md) and [chrome.md](chrome.md), said the way a machine checks it.
Every law in those five docs names the gate that fails when it breaks: `CANON` in `packages/gates/lib/canon.ts`, held by `bun run check canon`; `bun tools/check-canon.ts --list` prints each law's gates. A law lands with its gate, or not at all.
A rule is **held** (its gate fails the build) unless it says **pending**: its gate runs and reports, but fails nothing until the screens hold it (an entry in `PENDING_RULES`, `apps/web/e2e/_pendingRules.ts`). The loop: land the gate pending, burn the findings to zero, delete the entry.

Index: [Coverage](#coverage) R0 · [Strips and chips](#strips-and-chips) R2–R6, R37 · [Primitives](#primitives) R1, R7–R11, R15, R16, R18, R19, R23, R30, R32, R34, R36, R38, R39, R42–R50 · [Chrome](#chrome) R20, R22, R24, R27–R29 · [Measures](#measures) R12–R14, R17, R21, R25, R26, R31, R33, R35, R40, R41.

## Coverage

- **R0 Every layout gate sees every context** — one route list (`designRoutes`, `apps/web/e2e/_designTable.ts`): Reference, Listing, Peek, Live Sheet in Read and Edit, the wizards, Manage (the Training Bay, the Play hub, a Game, the invite screen, the Starter Set) and Play (player, Mediator, phone), at phone and page widths, signed in. `design-walk.e2e.ts` seeds once (`seedAccount`), walks once and hands each view to every layout gate, each holding a baseline of today's debt by route family; signed out, the gates walk the reference corpus (`REFERENCE_CORPUS`) with none. A route family with no entry, or a width missing, fails.

## Strips and chips

- **R2 Chips collapse to one side; never a gap** (C6, C7) — a strip's chips sit one gap apart, whatever their class, in a list as alone: no lane reserves room for a value another line has; an absent value draws nothing; a live reading reserves its maximum (`13/13`) in tabular figures.
- **R3 A chip is never clipped or dropped** (C3, C5) — only the name flexes; a header-only head's +MORE is the one exception, the cost right before it, the corner item (the cost, the action) rightmost on the name's line.
- **R37 Chips sit beside the name; the overflow goes to the body or +MORE** (C1a, C2b, C5) — a head's chips (its controls and status chips too) sit only on its name's line, and never make the head taller. On a header-only rendering (a Mini, a bodyless Compact, an index Compact with no lead, a head-only Full standing alone) what does not fit ends in +MORE; on a contentful one (a Compact with a body, a contentful Compact or Full, a Page, a contentful Mini, and a nested contentful with nothing under its name, which opens a body) it is the body's first row, left-aligned, in class order. The Tech Level, the cost and the slots after it never leave the head; the measures (Slots, SP, HP) stay next while they fit, ahead of the economy and traits; a name wraps only between words. One fit measures every head (`components/atoms/chipFill.ts`; a contentful Mini's `entity/parts/chipFit.ts`). A Page band on a phone lays its controls over its title by CSS alone, an index's search under it.
- **R4 Every strip is `Chips`, in class order** (C2, C2a, C10) — the Page band, index Compacts and unit Compacts too; each chip in its class's lane (`data-chip-class`), never back in the order, no read chip after the cost; budgets come from the form and its context, never a schema set; a header's summary repeats no child's fact; a foot band is text, never chips.
- **R5 A linked unit is a Mini, never a chip** (C11) — the same in Read and Edit; no role stamp ("Assigned Mech"), no Mini hung under a Compact. A unit's linked units lead its body as header-only Minis (`LinkedUnits`); a sheet's assigned units are one `MiniGrid` of contentful Minis (`AssignedUnits`). A relation that is no player unit stays a chip (a Game, a chassis); so do Play's crew line and a Mediator's seat, the named exception, where a pilot or mech is a relation chip.
- **R6 Nothing beside or under a strip** (C1, C8, E8) — state, uses and actions sit in the chip group, inlined last after the cost in fixed slots (state · uses · ⋯ or ✕), at the strip's Size; in Play the Mini's action is its Use there (§1.4); Details opens the Page. `Head`'s `controls` are that last slot for every Compact, TitledBox and card head; a Mini's control is a 24px target drawn a chip's height, 44px on touch. No control is the sibling right before or after an entity's list item.

## Primitives

- **R1 One section head, the solid Slab; one section, `SlabSection`** (§3.9, §5.7, §6.4) — a dashed head reads as user-made; a section is its Slab, one gap and its body (8px in a block, 12px on a Page), with the one fold (the Compact ghost `iconOnly` Button, its body hidden, never unmounted); its stamp, count chips and controls are Compact. `Slab` takes no `variant` or `style`.
- **R7 Entity markup lives only in `entity/`** (§0, §6.3, E15) — every host draws an entity, or stands in its frame, through the forms (`Entity`, `CompactFrame`, a Mini's `dim`); no `su-entity-(row|token)__` class and no `entity/parts` import outside `components/entity/` and its stylesheet. Play draws vitals with `VitalGauge`.
- **R8 One roll instrument** (§2.4, §3.4, E14) — `RollTableInstrument`, open on a Page, panel or resolve, no toggle; its stamp the one roll control, a result its marked row, the same mark for every outcome. Play's rolls are `PlayRollInstrument` or `PlayRollTable`; nested in a card a table is `RollMini`.
- **R9 No state colour fills a button** (§3.1, §3.3) — `buttonVariants` has no `danger`; destruction is rust behind `ConfirmDialog`.
- **R10 A surface sets its texture once** (§3.5.1, E13) — every brand-colour ground carries the speckle (`[data-grain]`) in every context, the Cockpit, its overlays and the Peek included; paper, cream, ink-deep, the page ground, buttons and fields never do.
- **R11 One Live Sheet grammar** (§0, §1.2) — pilot, mech, crawler and NPC share `LiveSheet`'s shell: Read | Edit where the viewer may write, actions in the band's corner, `SheetHero` with one vitals stack captioned in full words (`sheetVitals.model.ts`), one remove control (`SheetRemoveButton`), one unknown-item fallback (`UnknownItemLine`), one unit-out treatment, and the right sidebar of what the unit carries (`[data-sheet-aside]`, no fold in it; under the opening on a phone; an NPC carries nothing). Read has no field, stepper or empty section.
- **R15 One select mode** (EDL Modes) — at level 1 the ink ring and the "Selected" seal (on the head band's right, never on the frame); at level 2 the ring alone; one dim (`SELECT_DIM`), never a picker's own opacity; a single choice is a radio. A level-1 pick that is no entity is a `TitledBox` with `select`, solid.
- **R16 One component per chrome role** (§0, §3.12) — `WizShell`, `Input` / `Textarea` under `Field`'s one label, `BreadcrumbTrail`, `Pending`, one offline notice, the outlined `Button`, `Button href` for every link drawn as a button, one chip-well editor (`ChipListEditor`); a look no Button draws is its own atom (`ChipButton`, `TrackStep`, `ToggleCard`). No raw `<input>`, `<label>` or `<button>` outside the atoms. One verb per act (Move, Delete); Reference, never "SRD".
- **R18 Same content, same block** (E15, N5) — a rules block renders through one component everywhere: When Damaged is `WhenDamaged`, its field read only there (`damagedEffectOf` for everyone else).
- **R19 One ground signal** (K1) — the surface sets `data-ground` once; no `onInk`, `ground` or ink `surface` prop; paper on ink is `--color-paper-NN`, never a `color-mix`.
- **R23 Icons take their Size** (§5.11, K11) — 12, 14 or 18px from `iconSize`, stroke 2; ⋯ `Ellipsis`, close `X`, back `BackLabel` (`ChevronLeft` and the place's name), next `NextLabel` (the place's name and `ChevronRight`), a value's change `ArrowRight`; never an arrow or ellipsis character in copy.
- **R30 No left-rail borders** (§3.10) — colour rides in bands, stamps and gauges.
- **R32 Dashed means user-made** (§3.9) — or a Play reference unit, an edit field, an empty slot; never focus.
- **R34 The detail view is a Page, never a new tab** (E5) — no `window.open`.
- **R36 Stats render through `Stat`** (§3.7, §6.2) — never `<span>SP {n}</span>` nor a hand `dl`; a text value is a `Stat` too; a sentence says the number in words ("4 Hit Points").
- **R38 One subtle frame** (§4.3, E21) — every entity's frame (Compact, index Compact, contentful card, choice option, Mini pill, contentful Mini body, MiniGroup) is `--color-ink-40`, never solid ink or the entity's tone; only `--su-compact-frame` colours one (an ability tree's tier, a pattern's colour).
- **R39 One level deep is a Compact** (E6, N8) — on an entity's, a pattern's or a guide's Page, an entity one level deep is a Compact drawn open with all its content, never a Mini. Named exceptions stay Minis: a pattern's loadout on a chassis's or pattern's Page, a class's ability trees, a choice's entity options on its Page; a host's patterns are Minis a line wide, contentful with their description alone.
- **R42 A titled block is a TitledBox; a head alone draws no edge under it** — a document page's endpoint or command (`DocPanel`) and a roll table's name are cut-outs on their band, never a ruled strip; a bodyless Compact's bottom edge is transparent.
- **R43 A Page keeps its references whole** — its band's overflow row is its first row and stays under the band on a short Page (§1.7); an ability's Preceding and Next are header-only Compacts linking to their Pages; a table that prints only its 20 reads "Nothing happens." on 1–19; nothing in a reference page's content folds, outside a Peek; the chip fill never measures a title's screen-reader words.
- **R44 A Page's band wears its entity; nothing scrolls sideways** — gear its Tech Level (gear indexes TL1 blue), bio gear and its patterns mutant green, a Hybrid class its own brown (`class-hybrid`), never the tier rust, a creation guide its unit, Heat its red; a lettered Tech Level is spelled out (TECH LEVEL | BIO-CHASSIS); a control on a pink band is ink. No page is wider than a phone's window.
- **R45 A contentful Mini's tab is its body's paper** (E22) — its name sits on a paper tab that opens into its body's paper, no rule between. A no-chip index band's cut-out stands on its foot; a linked chip on a band's title line stands on its siblings' foot.
- **R46 A crawler bay reads as the book** — its body opens with Bay Info and When Damaged, then its choices, roll table, holdings and crew lead, on its Page and its sheet; on a sheet it is a contentful Mini holding those Minis (N3's named exception), only there are Bay Info named and its sections folded, and a writable bay's condition steps in Read; the Storage Bay's Scrap Pool and Crawler Hold head at the Mini Size. A crew lead's chip reads THE | role, its labels stand over their values (Keepsake, Motto), its HP is `VitalGauge size="mini"`, alike in Read and Edit. A crawler type's ability and NPC carry CRAWLER ABILITY and NPC chips, no band over them.
- **R47 A crawler tech level reads as the book** — its Page is the book's text in a plain Box, Tech Level and Structure Points on its band, the ladder of levels beside it. An index Compact with no lead whose budget leaves facts out is contentful: the head keeps its budget (TL, SP), the body the rest as chips.
- **R48 A class and its trees read as the class page** — the classes index heads Core and Hybrid each with a section; a path card wears its track (a Hybrid path the tier rust); a pilot sheet draws its class's trees as the class page does, untaken abilities greyed (an Advanced or Legendary tree only once taken), a taken ability with its full rules.
- **R49 A Live Sheet's opening** — its band says STARTER KIT or HOMEBREW where it came from, its chips Compact; its opening panels are one ink-40 hairline all round (`Box frame="hairline"`); a pilot's class is a Mini under the vitals; in a Game each assigned unit's Mini wears its owner chip (YOU, a crewmate's name, UNASSIGNED).
- **R50 A Game's people are social cards** — Compacts, their roles chips and their units Minis.

## Chrome

- **R20 A notice has one job** (K5) — toast for an act, `Banner` for a condition, `Message` for a consequence; one `Toaster` per document; one offline wording (`NotConnectedBanner`); Copied is the control's own swap (`useCopied`); no roll-tier colour outside the bot.
- **R22 One loading voice** (K6) — `Pending`, `Skeleton` or `RoutePending`, "Loading <thing>…" spelled by `loadingCopy` alone; a loading mark is the only `@keyframes`.
- **R24 Three dialog widths** (K7) — `ModalShell` narrow, default or page (28, 48, 72rem), no per-dialog `max-width`. One pick dialog (§6.6): `PickDialog`, narrow, its Compacts in select mode in one radio group, Cancel and the act in `DialogActions`; `RadioCardGroup` only there and in the wizards' steps.
- **R27 One empty state, one error per job** (K8) — `EmptyState` by place, `RecoveryPanel`, `NotFoundPanel`, `toast.error`; "Try again", never "Retry"; `role="alert"` only in `Message`, `RecoveryPanel` and `Banner`. There is no search page: `/search` 301s home.
- **R28 One focus ring** (K9) — `--focus-ring-shadow` on `:focus-visible`, everywhere, 3:1 on every ground; no `:focus` indicator of its own.
- **R29 Tabs, Segmented, Toggle by job** (K10) — `role="tab"` only in `Tabs`, `aria-pressed` only in `Segmented` and `Toggle`; no tab or segment label clipped or ellipsized.

## Measures

- **R12 One Size per line; 44px on touch; 11px at render; one stepper** (§4.6, §4.7, §6.2, §6.3, C1a) — every line's controls one height, menu items included, stamps and chips on that line too; no text under 11px as drawn; on a coarse pointer every menu item is 44px. One stepper (`StepButton`): a count steps through `Stat` in edit mode, a vital through `VitalGauge`'s `steppers`. Play's canvas never draws below its design size: under 1280 × 800 it draws its phone form.
- **R13 Tokens only** (§4.1–§4.4, §4.7) — no literal tracking, control height, ink `color-mix`, border width, radius or off-scale spacing; one frame rounding (`radius.card`, 3px) for every frame that is no entity; a component fit that is not spacing carries `design-tokens-ignore` and its reason.
- **R14 Caps through the stamp, `Button` and `capsLabel`** (§3.12, §4.2) — no other `uppercase`; 11px caps are snug.
- **R17 A Page has one rhythm** (§1.7, §7.1) — the first block within 24px of the band; 12px between level-1 blocks; every stat value framed 1px, every stat label 14px.
- **R21 One breakpoint ladder** (K2) — 40, 48, 64, 80rem, `width >=`, in media, container and JS alike; no px or `max-width` query.
- **R25 Motion is tokens** (K3) — `--dur-fast`, `--dur-base`, `--ease`; an `infinite` loop only for loading, under `no-preference`.
- **R26 One lift, one layer scale** (§4.4, K4) — `--shadow-lift`, 3px popups, z-index from `layer` (a literal above 3 fails).
- **R31 Value cells and seams** (§7.1, §7.2) — a value cell is paper with a 1px ink frame on every side (a paper rule on ink, the state colour under a state); a border-riding stamp centres within 1px on its line.
- **R33 A Peek is terminal** (§1.6, E16) — no button, link, input, tab stop or nested Peek on its plate; its one link is its card's name, to the entity's Page.
- **R35 The form fits the place** (E2, E3, E11, E19, §1.3, §6.6) — a list item is a Compact, index Compact or Mini; every item of a Live Sheet's lists is or holds a Mini or Compact (a carried thing the reference lacks is a Mini from `syntheticItemModel`); a Play list's line is a Mini; a `Lead` only on an SRD index; a chassis ability at level 1 only on its chassis or pattern Page and the mech hero, else a contentful Mini.
- **R40 A name sits at the left** (E22) — a name and its cut-out start at their head's left; only the art or the tier stands before them. Pending: `align`.
- **R41 A Button beside Tabs is their Size** (§4.7) — on the Tabs' line or in the band that holds them. Pending: `tab-size`.
