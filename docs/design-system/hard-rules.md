# The Hard Rules

Every canon law ([ruleset](ruleset.md), [EDL](entity-display-language.md), chips-and-actions, ledger, here) names the gate that fails when it breaks: `CANON` in `packages/gates/lib/canon.ts`, held by `bun run check canon`. A law lands with its gate.
A gate fails the build unless its rule is **pending** (`PENDING_RULES`, `e2e/_pendingRules.ts`): it reports under a ceiling till the screens hold it; at zero, delete it.

## Coverage

- **R0 Every layout gate sees every context** — one route list (`designRoutes`, `e2e/_designTable.ts`): every surface in Read and Edit, phone and page widths, signed in. `design-walk.e2e.ts` seeds and walks it once for every layout gate, a baseline per route family; signed out, the gates walk the reference corpus (`REFERENCE_CORPUS`).

## Primitives

- **R1 One section head, the solid Slab; one section, `SlabSection`** (§3.9, §5.7, §6.4) — a section is its Slab, one gap and its body (8px in a block, 12px on a Page), with the one fold (the Compact ghost `iconOnly` Button, its body hidden, never unmounted); its stamp, counts and controls are Compact. `Slab` takes no `variant` or `style`.
- **R7 Entity markup lives only in `entity/`** (§0, §6.3) — every host draws an entity, or stands in its frame, through the forms (`Entity`, `CompactFrame`, `Ledger`, a Mini's `dim`); no `su-entity-(row|token)__` class and no `entity/parts` import outside `components/entity/` and its stylesheet. Play draws vitals with `VitalGauge`.
- **R8 One roll instrument** (§2.4, §3.4) — `RollTableInstrument`, open on a Page, panel or resolve, no toggle; a roll bar in a contentful card, `RollMini` nested in one; its stamp the one roll control, a result its marked row, one mark for every outcome. Play's rolls are `PlayRollInstrument` or `PlayRollTable`.
- **R10 A surface sets its texture once** (§3.5.1, E13) — every brand-colour ground carries the speckle (`[data-grain]`) in every context, the Cockpit, its overlays and the Peek included; paper, cream, ink-deep, the page ground, buttons and fields never do.
- **R11 One Live Sheet grammar** (§0, §1.2) — pilot, mech, crawler, NPC, encounter share `LiveSheet`'s shell: Sheet | Edit for a writer | a pilot's Play, one Segmented, actions in the band's corner. A pilot, mech or crawler is its dashboard in both modes: the `Strip` masthead, vitals in full words, a side column of what it carries; an NPC is `SheetHero`. One remove control, one unknown-item fallback (`UnknownItemLine`), one unit-out look. Read has no field or empty section.
- **R11a One form a list** (L9) — Read and Edit draw each list in one form (`data-form`): Edit adds controls, never a second layout.
- **R15 One select mode** (EDL Modes, L7) — a Ledger's picked row is inked; a level-1 card wears the ink ring and the "Selected" seal (on the head band's right); at level 2 the ring alone; one dim (`SELECT_DIM`), never a picker's own opacity; a single choice is a radio. A level-1 pick that is no entity is a `TitledBox` with `select`, solid.
- **R16 One component per chrome role** (§0, §3.12) — `WizShell`, `Input` / `Textarea` under `Field`'s one label, `BreadcrumbTrail`, `Pending`, one offline notice, the outlined `Button`, `Button href` for every link drawn as a button, one chip-well editor (`ChipListEditor`); a look no Button draws is its own atom (`ChipButton`, `TrackStep`, `ToggleCard`, `PictureRadio`). No raw `<input>`, `<label>` or `<button>` outside the atoms and a `Ledger`'s rows (L7). One verb per act (Move, Delete); Reference, never "SRD".
- **R18 Same content, same block** (N5) — a rules block renders through one component everywhere: When Damaged is `WhenDamaged`, its field read only there (`damagedEffectOf` for everyone else).
- **R38 A frame wears its head's paint** (§4.3) — an entity's frame (Compact, index Compact, card, contentful Mini) and a headed box's (`TitledBox`, a Start Here step) is its band's paint (`--su-band`), never grey; with no band (a MiniGroup, a bare `Box`), `--color-ink-40`; `--su-compact-frame` overrides (a tree's tier). The cut-out stays whole.
- **R39 One level deep is a Compact** (N8) — on a Page, a nested entity is an open Compact, never a Mini. Exceptions: its actions are contentful Minis in a `MiniGrid` under an Actions Slab; a class's ability trees and a choice's options stay Minis; a list of options (a chassis's patterns, a guide step's) is a Ledger, each row linking to its Page.
- **R42 A titled block is a TitledBox; a head alone draws no edge under it** — a document page's endpoint or command (`DocPanel`) and a roll table's name are cut-outs, never a ruled strip; a bodyless Compact's bottom edge is transparent.
- **R43 A Page keeps its references whole** — its band's overflow row is its first row and stays under the band on a short Page (§1.7); an ability's Preceding and Next are header-only Compacts linking to their Pages; a table that prints only its 20 reads "Nothing happens." on 1–19; nothing in a reference page's content folds, outside a Peek; the chip fill never measures a title's screen-reader words. Its lore is titled Description.
- **R44 A Page's band wears its entity; nothing scrolls sideways** — gear but equipment its Tech Level, bio gear and its patterns mutant green, a Hybrid class its abilities' tier rust (`tier-hybrid`), a creation guide its unit, Heat its red, Crawler Downtime pink; a lettered Tech Level is spelled out (TECH LEVEL | BIO-CHASSIS); a control on a pink band is ink, bar its rust door, keylined in ink (§3.13). No page is wider than a phone's window.
- **R45 A Mini's name is a cut-out on its foot** (E22) — a cut-out shares its body's ground and border (`--cutout-ground`, `--cutout-border`), no edge on its open side: a header-only Mini's tab is its ground. A no-chip index band's cut-out stands on its foot; a band's linked chip on its siblings'.
- **R46 A crawler bay reads as the book** — its body opens with Bay Info and When Damaged, then its choices, roll table, holdings and crew lead, on its Page and sheet (a row of the bays' open Ledger, L9, opening to them at Mini Size); a writable bay's condition steps in Read. A crew lead's chip reads THE | role, its labels over their values, its HP a mini `VitalGauge` (a bare lead is header-only, HP a chip). A crawler type's ability and NPC carry CRAWLER ABILITY and NPC chips, no band. No crawler counts its bays.
- **R47 A crawler tech level reads as the book** — its Page is the book's text in a plain Box, Tech Level and Structure Points on its band, the ladder of levels beside it. An index Compact with no lead whose budget leaves facts out is contentful: the head keeps its budget (TL, SP), the body the rest as chips.
- **R48 A class and its trees read as the class page** — the classes index heads Core and Hybrid apart; a path card wears its track (a Hybrid path the tier rust); a pilot sheet draws its class's trees as the class page does, untaken abilities greyed and header-only (an Advanced or Legendary tree once taken), a taken one with its rules. At most three trees a line; on a sheet a column each, taken trees first.
- **R49 A Live Sheet's opening** — its band says STARTER KIT or HOMEBREW, its chips Compact; its opening panels one ink-40 hairline (`Box frame="hairline"`); a pilot's class is a Mini under the vitals; in a Game an assigned Mini wears its owner chip (MINE, a crewmate's name, UNASSIGNED); in Read a crawler's pilot wears its mech as a MECH chip.
- **R50 A Game's people are small cards in its page's sidebar** — header-only Minis, their roles chips; their units ride the Crew list.
- **R53 One sub-section rung** (§4.9, E25) — a section inside a section is the Mini Slab (`SlabSection size="mini"`), at chip size; no eyebrow, coloured bar or sentence-case head stands in for a section head, bar the book's contents chapters on the Reference home (`ContentsChapter`).
- **R54 A link looks like a link** — a standalone link is `Button href` or `InlineRef`, never bare `AppLink` text, and never larger than the note beside it.
- **R56 A Game's, a person's, a seat's, an index's name is never cut** (C2b) — the hub's doors drop to the body; chips wrap under.
- **R57 Class leads the pilot's Edit card** (§1.2) — its first row, above the photo: the name and a Change Class button.
- **R58 Intact is quiet** (C8) — only Damaged and Destroyed wear a tone; Repair shows only while damaged.
- **R59 A fact prints once** (C10) — no Uses trait beside a live Uses slot; granted gear's rules print under its ability only.
- **R60 A made choice reads as made** (§1.2) — a pick is a `CHOSEN | <pick>` chip; in Read a filled choice is its pick's Mini; in a picker a chip line under its row (L7); on its action's row its changed values.
- **R61 A box fits its content; a side column fills beneath** (§1.2, §1.7, §1.10) — opening panels are as tall as their content; from 64rem the Vitals head the sidebar; on a phone `HeroMore` follows them. A side column never ends in a void: what belongs with it sits under it, or the main column runs full width below.
- **R62 A unit Mini's acts ride its ⋯** (C8) — Change and Unassign are its menu's rows.
- **R65 An action says what it does, and all are there** (§1.4, R59) — Play's board lists them, never a sheet: every action of what the pilot and mech carry, the chassis ability and each companion's. A row's body is its effect alone: text the data tags `notInPlay` is left out, never reworded. Rows group by action type under Mini Slabs, Passive and untyped last.
- **R67 A guided build asks four things** (N6, R16, L9) — a wizard has at most four steps, each a decision; what the book only tells you is its step's cited `Callout`, never a step. A step's options are a Ledger in select, the pick's card beside it. The bar is a reserved strip, never over content: the budget's count, Cancel, one rust Next.
- **R70 Related units are one section** (R49) — a sheet's crew, mech, crawler and companions, each a link; in Read even empty, a place per slot.
- **R71 One rule-breaking mark** — a count over its cap (slots, Cargo, Inventory) wears `RuleBreakMark` by its count, in words; a save toasts what broke and by how much. A printed over-slot pattern wears none.

## Measures

- **R12 One height per Size; 11px at render; one stepper** (§4.6, §4.7, §6.2, §6.3, C1a) — every button and chip; a line one Size. Hidden hit areas: a linked chip's, strip Mini control's 24px (WCAG); a phone step's, remove's 44px. One stepper (`StepButton`): a count steps by `Stat` in edit, a vital by `VitalGauge`'s `steppers`.
- **R17 A Page has one rhythm** (§1.7, §7.1) — the first block ≤24px under the band; 12px between level-1 blocks; every stat value framed 1px, every stat label 14px (a short one 12).
- **R35 The form fits the place** (E2, E3, E11, E19, §1.3, §6.6) — a list item is a Compact, index Compact, Mini or Ledger row; every Live Sheet list item is or holds one (`syntheticItemModel` for a carried thing the reference lacks); a Play list's line is a Mini.
- **R68 A grid fills its rows** — a grid of items is the one fill grid (`.su-grid`): flex rows that wrap, a short last row growing to its width, to a cap (`--su-grid-max`); no empty track, no orphan cell. Masonry never has more columns than items. A pip track or table is no item grid.

## Chrome

What frames the content (ruleset §4; R16).

- **K1 One ground signal** — a surface declares its ground once (`data-ground="paper" | "ink"` on its frame); components read it in CSS, never through a prop. Paper on ink is `--color-paper-NN` alone.
- **K2 The breakpoint ladder** — 40, 48, 64 and 80rem (`breakpoint` in `tokens.ts`), written `(width >= …)`, in media and container queries and their JS. Exceptions: the stat column's cell count, a Compact's art by room (E10).
- **K3 Motion** — `--dur-fast` (120ms) for a state change, `--dur-base` (150ms) for a move, one easing, `--ease`. A loop is a loading mark (`--dur-loop`, 2s) or the home's slideshow (a slide out of turn hidden; a Pause), only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- **K4 Lift and layers** — one lift, `--shadow-lift`, for what floats (popup, Peek, toast, dialog); the focus ring is the other shadow. Popups are 3px (§4.4). Above the page, z-index is `layer`'s (`--z-*`); a literal above 3 fails.
- **K5 A notice has one job** — a toast reports an act just done, from the one `Toaster`; a `Banner` holds a lasting condition; a `FieldError` states a consequence where it applies. Offline is one notice, the bar's `SyncBadge`. Copied is the copy control's `Check` swap, never a toast. No notice wears a roll-tier colour (§3.4).
- **K6 One loading voice** — `Pending` inside a screen, `Skeleton` for a shape known ahead, `RoutePending` for a route; "Loading <thing>…", spelled by `loadingCopy` alone; only K3's loops are `@keyframes`.
- **K7 Three dialog widths** — `ModalShell` is narrow (28rem), default (48rem) or page (72rem: the Page, the searcher); no per-dialog `max-width`. One pick dialog (§6.6): `PickDialog`, narrow, its Compacts in select in one radio group (toggles for several), Cancel and the act in `DialogActions`; `RadioCardGroup` only there and in the wizards' steps.
- **K8 One empty state, one error per job** — `EmptyState` by place (a stamp on a Page or sheet section, quiet in app chrome, inline in a framed list), "No <things> yet."; a crash is `RecoveryPanel`, a missing record `NotFoundPanel`, a failed act `toast.error`; "Try again", never "Retry"; `role="alert"` only in `FieldError`, `RecoveryPanel` and `Banner`.
- **K9 One focus ring** — every focusable thing shows `--focus-ring-shadow` on `:focus-visible`, in ink, 3:1 on every ground; no outline, underline, dashed, colour-only or `:focus` ring.
- **K10 Tabs, Segmented, Toggle, Switch by job** — `Tabs` pick a view (`role="tab"` only there), `Segmented` sets a value and `Toggle` is one press (`aria-pressed` only in those two), `Switch` a setting; no tab or segment label clipped.
- **K11 Icons take their Size** (§5.11) — 12, 14 or 18px from `iconSize` (Mini, Compact, Full), stroke 2; ⋯ `Ellipsis`, close `X`, back `BackLabel` and next `NextLabel` (a chevron and the place's name), a value's change `ArrowRight`; never an arrow or ellipsis character in copy.
- **K12 Link previews** — a page's 1200 × 630 card is its Page in small: band and name, chips beside it, cite and path at the foot; canon solid, homebrew dashed; no type under 34px; theme-color is the band.
- **K13 Chrome matches** — header and footer: `--color-paper` on `--color-ink-deep`; the rail: its band, words `--su-on-kind`.
- **K14 Every control in reach** — every control sits inside its frame at every width; nothing invisible takes its press.
- **K15 Table notices pin as one** (K5) — over every screen: the Stop Sign, then one strip: one notice, the count, Dismiss all; Play fills the rest.
- **K16 Rules beside the board** — from `lg` Play's Rules are a pane, never a dialog.
- **R52 One muted note** — a lede, hint or note is `Text variant="note"`; no style object pairs a muted ink with a font size.
