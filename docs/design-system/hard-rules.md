# The Hard Rules

Each law of [ruleset.md](ruleset.md), [EDL](entity-display-language.md), [chips-and-actions.md](chips-and-actions.md) and [chrome.md](chrome.md) that a machine can check, with the gate that holds it.
Status: agreed. A rule is **held** (its gate fails the build), **partial** (a gate covers some surfaces), **pending** (its gate runs and reports, but fails nothing until the screens hold it: an entry in `PENDING_RULES`, `apps/web/e2e/_pendingRules.ts`, or a ratchet's baseline) or **planned** (the gate is named, not built).
The loop: define it here, land its gate with a baseline, burn the baseline to zero, then mark it held. Evidence: [Consistency Rulebook](https://claude.ai/artifact/4dbxMJqNv6Ti3Zwjh6jG1w).

## Strips and chips

- **R2 Chips collapse to one side; never a gap** (C6, C7; owner, 2026-10-20: "chips should *always* collapse spacing to the right or left, never have gaps") — a strip's chips sit one gap apart, whatever their class, in a list as alone: no lane reserves room for a value another line has, an absent value draws nothing; readings are tabular.
  Gate: held. `Chips` draws only the lanes it fills, each `display: contents`; there is no lane-width logic to reserve a column. `_chipStrips.ts` (`chip-strips.e2e.ts`) fails a strip whose gaps differ by more than a pixel (`gap:`), between classes too. `Stat` reserves a live reading's maximum (`13/13`) in tabular figures.
- **R3 A chip is never clipped or dropped** (C3, C5) — only the name flexes; a header-only head's +MORE is the one exception.
  Gate: held, `_chipStrips.ts` (`chip-strips.e2e.ts`, signed out and over R0's routes) fails a chip outside its strip's visible box (`clip`), a header-only Mini's chips on two lines (`lines`), a +MORE short of its strip's bottom line's end or dropping its cost (`more`), a cost not right before that +MORE (`cost`), and a head whose corner item (the cost, the action) is off its name's line or not rightmost there (`fill`); the seeded Game's long name holds the Training Bay's Game chip inside its Compact (#1616).
- **R37 Chips sit beside the name; the overflow goes to the body or +MORE** (C1a, C2b, C5; owner, 2026-10-20) — 1. a head's chips (its controls and status chips too) only ever sit beside its name, on the name's line; 2. they never make the head taller; 3. on a header-only rendering (a Mini, a bodyless Compact, an index Compact with no lead, a head-only Card standing alone) what does not fit ends the strip in a +MORE chip, the cost right before it; 4. on a contentful rendering (a Compact with a body, a Card, a Page, a contentful Mini, and a nested Card with nothing under its name, which opens a body for it: owner, 2026-10-20, an Oil Bomb's chips "should create and spill into the content") it is the body's first row, left-aligned, in class order. The Tech Level, the cost and the slots after it never leave the head; the measures (Slots, SP, HP) stay next while they fit, ahead of the economy and the traits (owner, 2026-10-20: "TL and slots should be in the header, others should flow beneath"); a name wraps only between words. A Page's band on a phone lays its controls (not its stamps) over its title by CSS alone, an index's search under it (owner, 2026-10-20).
  Gate: held. One fit for every head (`components/atoms/chipFill.ts`: `Head`, `index Compact`, `ChapterBand`; a contentful Mini's is `entity/parts/chipFit.ts`, on the same `overflowPlan`): what does not fit is `data-fill-out`, copied into the body's `ChipOverflowRow` or stood for by `MoreChip` (`components/atoms/chipOverflow.tsx`); `chipFill.test.ts`, `chipFit.test.ts`. `_chipStrips.ts` (`chip-strips.e2e.ts`, signed out and over R0) fails `taller` (a fill head or a contentful Mini's head taller as drawn than with its chips laid on one line beside the name, the name held at its width: measured in the browser), `overflow` (a chip that rose above its name's line, a contentful Mini's head on two lines, and an overflow row, `[data-chip-overflow]`, that is not its body's first row, not left-aligned or holds the cost or the Tech Level) and `more-contentful` (a +MORE on a contentful Mini or on a head with an overflow row); `_stripControls.ts` reads the overflow row as its head's chip group.
- **R4 Every strip is `Chips`, in class order** (C2, C2a, C10) — the Page band, index Compacts and unit Compacts included; budgets from the form, never a schema set; a header's summary repeats no child's fact; a foot band is text, never chips.
  Gate: held. `Chips` draws every strip (a Mini's, a Compact's, Card's or `Head`'s, the Page band, an index Compact's, a Slab's summary), each chip in its class's lane (`data-chip-class`); `_chipStrips.ts` fails a chip in no lane (`unclassed`), a strip that goes back in the class order (`order`), a read chip after the cost (`cost`) and a chip in a `[data-foot-band]` (`foot`), over the SRD pages with no allowlist and over R0 with no baseline. `UnitRow.test.tsx` pins `unitRowModel`'s budget (a roster keeps the rank and the lead vital); `slabSummary.test.tsx` pins C10 and the two-chip summary. A index Compact's budget is a compact head's in its kind's context (`budgetFacts`), no schema set.
- **R5 A linked unit is a Mini, never a chip, everywhere** (C11; owner, 2026-10-20: "References to other units should always be compact or mini references"; round 4: "no linked units should be chips. all should be entities") — the same in Read and Edit; no role stamp, no Mini hung under a Compact.   Gate: held. `copy/banned-variant` bans the role stamps "Assigned Mech" and "Assigned Pilot" (`packages/gates/rules/copyTerms.ts`); `relation-chips.e2e.ts` (`_relationChips.ts`) over R0 fails an entity in a Compact's body outside its linked units (`[data-entity-linked]`) or a Mini hung under a Compact, no baseline. A Starter Set Compact's "Copy to…" is a Mini chip in its last slot opening the destinations (`CopyStarterMenu`, a `HeaderMenu`), the Compact a covered link (a link Compact with relations is covered, its relations linking above the cover); the Mediator's All proposals is the Propose dock's head control; a saved pattern's Build With This, an NPC pick's Carry (a `Toggle`), the NPC template Mini's Change and a searcher rail Mini's ✕ are their entity's last chip; a crew slot's assigned NPC card carries Open and Unassign as its controls, a linked crew inset its Open. A `ReferenceEntityCard` has no rail: its `controls` are its head's last slot, a `stepper` or `status` control and a read card's condition (`StatusBadge`, ruleset §2.3) its state slot (`cardStrip.tsx`, `cardStrip.test.tsx`); a crawler bay's Scan or Dock, Repair, fold and condition all ride its head.
