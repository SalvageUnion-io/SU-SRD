# The Hard Rules

Each law of [ruleset.md](ruleset.md), [EDL](entity-display-language.md), [chips-and-actions.md](chips-and-actions.md) and [chrome.md](chrome.md) that a machine can check, with the gate that holds it.
Status: agreed. A rule is **held** (its gate fails the build), **partial** (a gate covers some surfaces) or **planned** (the gate is named, not built).
The loop: define it here, land its gate with a baseline, burn the baseline to zero, then mark it held. Evidence: [Consistency Rulebook](https://claude.ai/artifact/4dbxMJqNv6Ti3Zwjh6jG1w).

Run over it: `sibling-heights.e2e.ts`, `chip-strips.e2e.ts` and `nesting-depth.e2e.ts`, each with a `BASELINE` of today's debt by route family and rule. ## Strips and chips

- **R2 In a list of Rows, every chip class is a column** (C6, C7; a Token packs right) — siblings share lane edges; an absent value leaves its cell empty; readings are tabular.
  Gate: held. A line's strip (a Row's, Token's or Card's head) keeps every class's lane; `chipLanes.ts` sets each lane's width on its list (`--su-lane-<class>`, the widest down the list) where its lines are one width, from `Chips` or, on a static page, `islands.client.ts`; a Row packs below 40rem (a container query), a Token on a phone. `Stat` reserves a live reading's maximum (`13/13`) in tabular figures. `_chipLanes.ts` (run by `chip-strips.e2e.ts`) fails a class whose left edges differ down a column of a list with room, over the SRD pages with no allowlist and over R0 with no baseline.
- **R3 A chip is never clipped or dropped** (C3, C5) — only the name flexes; a contentful Token's +MORE is the one exception.
  Gate: held, `_chipStrips.ts` (`chip-strips.e2e.ts`, signed out and over R0's routes) fails a chip outside its strip's visible box (`clip`), a header-only Token's chips on two lines and a contentful Token's on three (`lines`; a Row's or card's head stacks its chips up the band beside the name on as many lines as they take, raising them onto the band above it only when one chip is wider than the room the name leaves: `useChipsFit`), a +MORE outside a contentful Token, short of its strip's bottom line's end or dropping its cost (`more`), a cost not right before that +MORE (`cost`), and a contentful Token's line under another that is not full (`fill`); the seeded Game's long name holds the Training Bay's Game chip inside its Row (#1616).
  A contentful Token's head (N3) wraps its compact budget beside the name, bottom-heavy (the bottom line full, the cost in its corner); past two lines `chipFit.ts` draws the chips that fit, in class order, keeping the cost and its slots, and ends the bottom line in the cost, then +MORE, a button beside the head's link that opens its Peek. With no script every chip wraps (no +MORE).
- **R4 Every strip is `Chips`, in class order** (C2, C2a, C10) — the Page band, Tiles and unit Rows included; budgets from the form, never a schema set; a header's summary repeats no child's fact; a foot band is text, never chips.

- **R5 A linked unit is a relation chip** (C11) — the same in Read and Edit; no role stamp, no Token under a Row.
