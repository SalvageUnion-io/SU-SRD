# The Ledger

A list you compare or pick from is one table, between the book's banded table and the Compacts ([EDL](entity-display-language.md) E2). A row is no strip: [chips-and-actions.md](chips-and-actions.md) does not lay it. Code: `components/entity/Ledger`, `OpenLedger`.

- **L1 One table a list, unframed** — one `Ledger` (a sheet's: its open form, `OpenLedger`): the head strip, the rows, and a 2px ink rule closing them; no frame. No row wears a band, frame or tone of its own. An open row opens its rules in place under itself (its chevron); a sub-group is a small caps label on a hairline.
- **L2 The head strip** — the band's colour, speckled (R10). The list's title is a paper cut-out tab at its left (E22), with no count: the tab is the section head, and no Slab stands over a Ledger. A tab that would repeat its page's title reads Name, still a cut-out. Each column's label is a cut-out too, on one line; a picker ends the strip with what it asks (`CHOOSE | 1`) or its budget. On an SRD index, and nowhere else, the tab and each stamp sort by their column (§3.13's one exception), the sorted one marked by a chevron.
- **L3 Rows** — a Mini's line, banded paper and `--color-band-cream`, a 1px hairline between. A quiet line is one line, cut with "…"; nothing else is cut, nor anything on a Page: a prose column wraps.
- **L4 The name** — first, in condensed caps (`capsLabel`), the row's link, with Peek (a loadout's parts link too). The only stamp on a row is its mark (STARTING) or rank (TL).
- **L5 Columns** — a value is a plain condensed tabular numeral in a fixed column, in the stat order (§2.6): a list drops a column no row fills, never moves one, and a stat that is a pick's price keeps its place and name. An absent value is `—`. Words that place the entity (tree, crew lead, chassis, maker) follow the name; one quiet line (traits, action names, a loadout) follows DMG; the activation cost is last. A stat fewer than a quarter of the rows carry is left to the Page. A cell is no `Stat` and wears no frame (§7.1): its label is its head stamp.
- **L6 No foot** — a list draws no sources cut-out: its rows' Pages cite them. A note (what a pick costs) or a total line sits under the rows.
- **L7 A picker is the same Ledger** — in select: each row a radio, the picked row filled ink, its values paper, its name on a small paper tab; no seal. Where an item can be taken more than once the row ends in a −, count, + stepper (`StepButton`), the count marks it picked and the row its card shows is lined in ink; a row that cannot be afforded or fitted wears the one dim. A made choice is a `CHOSEN` chip line under the picked row (R60). The pick's card stands contentful beside the list, under its row on a phone. The row's radio in its label and an index's sorting stamp are the Ledger's own markup (R16); keyboard focus is the one ring, on the frame (K9).
- **L8 A phone** — keeps the name, the stamp, at most two figure columns and the foot; a list with no figures puts its quiet line under the name, two lines at most. No sideways scroll (R44).
- **L9 Where** — every SRD index, which opens on its List (the classes' Core and Hybrid as two, their tabs the group names; a list of roll tables too); a Page's list of options (a chassis's patterns, book or homebrew); search results, the highlighted one inked like a picked row; every wizard step's options; a Training Bay shelf (a relation there, its pilot or Game, is a plain link in its column). A Ledger of player-made things that could pass for canon (patterns, NPCs) wears one dashed HOMEBREW stamp beside its tab and credits its makers in a column (§3.9): never a dashed frame or row, and never on a shelf of your own units, whose live vital is a column. A list of mixed kinds wears an ink band and a Kind column. A Live Sheet's lists (systems, modules, gear, generic abilities, bays; trees R48) are open Ledgers; Play's lines stay Minis (§1.3). Catalog stays index Compacts.
- **L10 Band** — an SRD index's band holds its name and List | Catalog only; search, facets and filter status form one bar atop the list.
