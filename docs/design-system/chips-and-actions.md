# Chips and Actions

The law for what an entity strip or titled header shows, and where; it amends [EDL](entity-display-language.md) E17, and extends [ruleset.md](ruleset.md) §4.7 to chips. Design: [Mini & Chip Grammar](https://claude.ai/artifact/KgnbEv1HTJ4sYoNxiyhffj): dense, calm strips that never shift the view.

## The strip

A strip is a Mini, a Compact or Full head, or a TitledBox or Slab header.

1. **Zones** — identity (tier or number, the name) · information (chips, the actions last among them).
2. **Classes** — the read chips, in one order: mark (note, HOMEBREW), rank (TL), measure (in the stat order, ruleset §2.6: Slots, SV, SP or HP), economy (action type, RNG, DMG), traits, relation (what it is built on or linked to: `CHASSIS | Mule`, `PILOT | Bonesaw`, `CRAWLER | #430`, `GAME | Rust Run`), cost.
   Then the slots (C8): state (a live reading, condition, quantity), uses, ⋯ or ✕. The cost sits at the seam: the last read chip, read or live.
3. **Budgets** — Mention: none. Mini: rank, one context lane, cost, state; its relations are its Peek's.
   Compact (and a contentful Mini): rank, the context lane, the economy (action type, facets), traits, relations, cost, state; other measures are Full's.
   Full, Page and Peek: every class (in a Peek, relation and action chips are inert, ruleset §1.6). TitledBox and Slab: at most two summary chips, totals and caps of what they hold.

## Rules

- **C1a** One Size per strip: its chips and controls share one height and one centre line, Mini on a Mini, Compact on any other head.
  Controls and status chips (a condition, a stepper, Use, ⋯) are the head's chips: they live in the header under C2b's fill, never on a line of their own.
- **C2** Every strip, the Page band, index and unit Compacts included, is drawn by `Chips`, in the class order, each chip in its class's lane (`data-chip-class`); a form drops classes, never reorders them; no read chip follows the cost. Budgets come from the form and its context, never a schema set.
- **C2b** Chips sit beside the name, on its line, filling right to left from the bottom right, the corner chip (the cost, else the last slot or the Page's action) last; they never make the head taller. What does not fit ends a header-only rendering (a Mini, a bodyless Compact, the index layout with no lead, a lone head-only Full) in +MORE (C5), and on a contentful one (a Compact with a body, a contentful Compact, Full or Mini, a Page; a nested contentful with nothing under its head opens a body) is the body's first row, left-aligned, in class order.
  The cost, the Tech Level and the slots never leave the head; the measures (Slots, SP, HP) stay next while they fit; the economy and traits fill what is left from the end (one too wide leaves, the next is still tried). A Mini's or Compact's name gives way to half its line, then ends in "…" (C5), save a whole name (R56); a Full's or Page's wraps between words, and where its widest word leaves too little, a contentful head's unclassed items (a band's action, a type stamp) join the first row; where even that breaks a word, the name takes the band's width and all but the cost and slots are the first row.
  One fit, measured once (`components/atoms/chipFill.ts`, a contentful Mini's `entity/parts/chipFit.ts`); until it measures, a head is laid as it most often ends. On a phone a Page band keeps its controls above its title, flush right, by CSS alone; an index band's search and filters follow its title on the page ground, the search a line of its own, Filters the line's width. A Page or sheet band's chips are Compact, short labels (TL, SP, SV) on a phone, full words on a page.
- **C3** Budgets are static: no chip is added, dropped, moved or clipped by width, content or hover; a strip never hides its overflow. What a Mini leaves out, its Peek shows. The one exception is C5's +MORE.
- **C4** A Mini's or Compact's context lane is named by its container (else its kind's: gear `loadout`, a thing you do `play`, an actor `roster`), from a closed list: `loadout` (Slots; a unit's SP), `play` (action type or `ACTIONS | n`), `inventory` (quantity over 1), `roster` (the vital, `HP | 10/10`), `browse` (none). The model maps a context to a fact per kind; no component branches on schema.
- **C5** Only an entity's name flexes: a Mini's or Compact's that will not fit ends in "…" and Peeks whole; a Full's, a Page's, a Tab's, a Peek's and a tool panel's title (Propose a change) wrap. No chip, stamp or control shrinks, clips, scales or is cut to "…".
  A header-only rendering's chips are one row (C2b); what does not fit ends it in a +MORE chip (no count), the cost kept right before it, the corner item rightmost on the name's line, and the entity (its Peek, its Page) shows the rest. A contentful rendering never shows +MORE: its overflow is C2b's body row.
- **C6** Chips collapse to one side, one gap apart, whatever their class, in a list as alone: no lane is a column (bar a Ledger's, L5), an absent value draws nothing; a line's strip is its own. A live reading reserves its maximum's width (`13/13`), in tabular figures.
- **C8** A strip is identity, then its chips, the actions inlined last, at its Size, in fixed slots: state · uses · ⋯ or ✕; in Play the Mini's action is its Use there (ruleset §1.4). `Head`'s `controls` are that slot for every Compact, TitledBox and card head; nothing sits beside a strip or is a sibling of an entity's list item, and nothing expands under it: Details opens the Page.
  A state's follow-up (Repair) opens from the state slot; at most two visible, then ⋯; never hover-only. A Mini with actions is a `div` whose name cut-out is the link and Peek trigger, stretched over the pill, so no button sits in a link.
- **C9** A mode swaps what fills a slot (a read chip, a control, a seal), never its size.
- **C10** A header's summary never repeats a child's fact or its parent's summary; Slab and TitledBox take it as one `SlabCount` list. A foot band is text, never chips.
- **C11** A related entity is a relation chip, label first, its value Peeking and opening it (a Game, which has no Peek, links to its page): never a loose stamp ("Assigned: X") or a Mini beside the strip. Read and Edit show the same chip; an absent relation draws nothing, never "Unassigned". An ability's tree is its one relation chip; its Preceding and Next are its Page's nested references.
  A linked player unit is a Mini, never a chip, with no role stamp and no Mini hung under a Compact: a unit's linked units lead its body as header-only Minis (`LinkedUnits`), a sheet's assigned units are one `MiniGrid` of contentful Minis (`AssignedUnits`). Play's crew line and a Mediator's seat stay chips. Boarded, a pilot and mech are the mech's Mini with a PILOT chip; else the pilot's with MECH.
- **C13** The Training Bay shows only what is yours: your own units, in a Game or not, and the crawler of a Game you run; never a crewmate's or an unassigned unit (the Game's page has those), read via `useOwnUnits`, `useKeptCrawlers`. A unit's Game is its `GAME | name` chip (C11), never a filter; no Games list.
