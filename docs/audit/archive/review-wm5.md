## Findings

### FINDING 1: He2 Coolant Flush — page number discrepancy

**File:** `modules.json`
**Field:** `page`
**JSON value:** `197`
**PDF value:** The He2 Coolant Flush appears on page **205** in the PDF (in the Tech 5 section).

The module appears on page 205 alongside other Tech 5 modules (Adv. Targeting Array, Holo Projector, Multi-Optics). Page 197 is in the Tech 2 section of modules. The JSON `page` field of `197` appears incorrect.

**Severity:** Medium (wrong page reference)

---

### FINDING 2: Augmented Crawler — Union Crawler A.I. hit points

**File:** `crawlers.json`
**Field:** `npc.hitPoints`
**JSON value:** `0`
**PDF value:** The PDF on page 216 does not explicitly state a hit point value for the A.I. (it is a computer system, not a person). However, all other crawler NPCs have `4` HP. Having `0` is either intentional (A.I. isn't a person) or a data entry omission.

**Severity:** Low (possibly intentional design choice — A.I. is not a physical NPC)

---

### FINDING 3: Storage Bay — page number

**File:** `crawler-bays.json`
**Field:** `page`
**JSON value:** `221`
**PDF value:** The Storage Bay content begins on page **222** in the PDF. Page 221 has the Command Bay and Mech Bay. The Storage Bay heading appears at the top of page 222.

**Severity:** Low (off by one page)

---

### FINDING 4: Trading Bay — NPC content text discrepancy

**File:** `crawler-bays.json` (Trading Bay entry)
**Field:** `npc.content[0].value`
**JSON value:** `"The Trading Bay allows for Scrap as well as Intact Mech Chassis, Systems, and Modules to be traded. As the Union Crawler travels across the wasteland, this represents the various waste- landers and other folk who stop by the Union Crawler with wares to trade back and forth."`
**PDF value (p222-223):** The NPC description in the PDF is separate from the bay description. The PDF states: "The Trading Bay is operated and maintained by the Union Traders, the most experienced of whom is known as the Operator."

The JSON `npc.content` for Trading Bay contains the bay's general description rather than the NPC-specific description that other bays use. Compare to other bays which have NPC content like "The Mech Bay is operated and maintained by Mech Engineers, the most experienced of whom is known as the Greaser." The Trading Bay's NPC content block has the bay's general description instead of the NPC-specific text.

**Severity:** Medium (content placed in wrong field — bay description is in NPC content)

---

### FINDING 5: Medium distance — formatting/text issues

**File:** `distances.json`
**Field:** `content[0].value` and `content[1].value`
**JSON value for paragraph 1:** `"You are able to clearly see and identify your target at this Range"`
**JSON value for paragraph 2:** `" You are in Range to fire many standard weapons such as the 30mm Autocannon or Green Laser."`

**Issues:**
1. Paragraph 1 is missing a period at the end: should be `"You are able to clearly see and identify your target at this Range."` (with period)
2. Paragraph 2 has a leading space: `" You are in Range..."` — the space before "You" should be removed
3. The PDF actually presents this as continuous text: "You are able to clearly see and identify your target at this Range. You are in Range to fire many standard weapons such as the 30mm Autocannon or Green Laser."

**Severity:** Low (formatting artifacts, missing period)

---

### FINDING 6: Mech Appearance roll table — page number

**File:** `roll-tables.json`
**Field:** `page`
**JSON value:** `94`
**PDF value:** The Appearance Table appears on page **208** alongside the Quirks Table.

The table content on page 208 of the PDF matches the JSON data exactly. The page reference of 94 appears to point to an earlier mention in the Mech creation section, but the actual table with all 20 entries is on page 208.

**Severity:** Low (page points to earlier reference rather than table location; the table appears in both places)
